# LockerBuddy

A gentle to-do locker. Check off tasks on a magnet board and each one lands in your binder with the date and time.

- 4-digit combo lock
- Color-coded year and month binder tabs
- Friendly reminders, no guilt
- Works offline and installs to your home screen
- No account, no server. Your data stays on your device.
